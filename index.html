01 — Introduction

The Legendary Tournament

Every four years, the world stops to watch a single tournament.

The FIFA World Cup is the most-watched sporting event on Earth, contested not by clubs but by nations. Since the first tournament in Uruguay in 1930, it has grown from a small gathering of thirteen teams into a global spectacle that draws billions of viewers and brings entire countries to a standstill. This page traces that story — how the tournament grew, where it has been played, who made it legendary, and where it goes next.

02 — How it grew

The History of the World Cup

The World Cup of today is almost unrecognisable from the one that began in 1930. The number of teams has more than tripled, the format has been reinvented several times, and the way the game is played has changed the rhythm of goals. These charts trace the growth of the tournament itself — the stage on which every later story unfolds.

Australia at the World Cup

The Road to 2026

In 2026, the World Cup becomes bigger than ever — the first hosted by three nations, the first with forty-eight teams, and the first to guarantee every confederation a place at the finals. From thirteen nations in 1930 to forty-eight in 2026, the story is still being written.
